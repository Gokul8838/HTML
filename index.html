Gokul

Gokul is a small town in the Mathura district of Uttar Pradesh, India. It is known for its religious significance, particularly in Hinduism, as it is associated with the childhood of Lord Krishna. Gokul is a popular pilgrimage site and attracts devotees from all over the country.

Gokulakrishnan is also called as Lord Krishna's birthplace and is believed to be the place where he spent his early years. The town is dotted with temples and shrines dedicated to Lord Krishna, making it a significant destination for spiritual seekers.
